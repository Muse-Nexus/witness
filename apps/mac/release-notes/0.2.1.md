Witness for Mac reads incoming texts on this Mac and sends individual messages that might be kind to the Witness service you choose. That service decides what to keep.

**What changed in 0.2.1**
The setup now says, before Messages access, that each message sent includes the sender's phone number or email, even with names off. The Contacts permission prompt also explains that the selected sender's name is uploaded when names are on. These are disclosure corrections; capture behavior is unchanged from the previous Mac release.

**What leaves your Mac**
Only messages that might be kind, one at a time. Each goes with the sender's phone number or email, when it was sent, the service (iMessage, SMS or RCS), whether the chat was direct or a group, and Messages' own ID for it. With names on, the name you saved for the sender goes too. Your Witness keeps just the kind ones. Your own messages, whole conversations, attachments and your address book are not uploaded by the Mac collector.

Your selected Witness service processes these messages. Evidence is encrypted when stored, not end-to-end encrypted: its operator can decrypt it. The hosted preview is invite-only. Its optional AI check is currently on and sends borderline message text to Anthropic, along with the subject and sender name if present. Before connecting, read the service's Privacy page and decide whether you agree to that processing. Self-hosted services have their own configuration. The permission flow for this AI check needs further work before hosted access is broadened.

**Install or update**
1. Read the upload disclosure above before installing. An existing completed setup resumes capture when Witness opens; this update does not ask for consent again.
2. If Witness is running, choose Pause before Quit to keep capture paused while you review the updated Settings. Then quit Witness.
3. Open `Witness-0.2.1.dmg` and replace Witness in Applications.
4. Open Witness. New setups choose the Witness web address and paste a Mac key from Set up → Texts & photos. Existing settings and the saved key use the same locations as 0.2.0. In Settings, read Messages access and Names before choosing Resume if you paused.
5. Allow Full Disk Access only if you choose automatic Messages capture. Names from Contacts are optional. Choose how far back to look; Pause and Quit stop capture.
