Witness for Mac reads the texts people send you in Messages on this Mac and sends the ones that might be kind to your Witness, one at a time. Your Witness keeps the kind ones.

**Install**
1. Download `Witness-0.2.0.dmg`, open it and drag Witness to Applications.
2. Open Witness. In its first step, paste your Witness address and a Mac key (Witness → Set up → Texts & photos → Create a Mac key).
3. Allow Full Disk Access when it asks. It needs it to read Messages; nothing else on the Mac is read.
4. Choose how far back to look: the last 30 days, the last year, or everything. You can look further back later in Settings.

**What leaves your Mac**
Only messages that might be kind, one at a time. Each one goes with the sender's phone number or email, when it was sent, the service (iMessage, SMS or RCS), whether the chat was direct or a group, and Messages' own ID for it, so nothing is kept twice. If you turn names on, the name you saved for the sender goes too. Your Witness keeps just the kind ones. Everything else stays on your Mac: your own messages, whole conversations, attachments and your address book.

**Details**
- macOS 14 or later. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
- A long look back goes a few messages at a time, and nothing is kept twice.
- Pause any time from the menu bar. If Full Disk Access is turned off, Witness pauses and resumes by itself when it is back.
